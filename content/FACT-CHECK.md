## Artemis Fowl — Eoin Colfer

64 questions · character: 10, detail: 35, in_which_book: 14, sequence: 5 · confidence: school: 12, sourced: 52

### Fact sheet

> Artemis Fowl II is a 12-year-old genius from an Irish family of criminals. His father has disappeared and the family fortune is gone, so Artemis makes a bold plan: steal the secrets of the fairies, kidnap one, and demand gold as ransom. He captures Captain Holly Short of the fairy police (the LEP). The fairies surround his home, Fowl Manor, freeze time around it, and try everything to rescue her — but Artemis has a plan for almost everything.

**Characters**

- [ ] **Artemis Fowl II** (Main character): 12-year-old genius and heir to a criminal family; wants fairy gold to rebuild the family fortune. _[SS]_
- [ ] **Butler** (Bodyguard): Artemis's huge, highly trained bodyguard; his family has protected the Fowls for generations. _[SS-CL]_
- [ ] **Juliet** (Butler's sister): Butler's younger sister; a maid at Fowl Manor who looks after Artemis's mother. _[SS-CL]_
- [ ] **Angeline Fowl** (Artemis's mother): Has stayed in her room, unwell, since her husband disappeared. _[SS]_
- [ ] **Artemis Fowl I** (Artemis's father): Went missing about a year earlier on a shipping venture to Russia. _[SS-CL]_
- [ ] **Captain Holly Short** (Kidnapped fairy): An elf; the first female officer in the LEP's recon unit. Artemis kidnaps her. _[SS; WP]_
- [ ] **Commander Julius Root** (LEP commander): Red-faced, short-tempered boss of LEP Recon; leads the siege of Fowl Manor. _[SS-CL; WP]_
- [ ] **Foaly** (LEP tech expert): A paranoid centaur who runs the fairies' technology; wears a tinfoil hat. _[SS-CL]_

**Settings**

- [ ] **Fowl Manor, Ireland**: The Fowl family mansion; Holly is held prisoner here. _[SS]_
- [ ] **Haven City**: The fairies' city, deep underground. _[WP]_
- [ ] **Tara, Ireland**: Where Holly goes to do the Ritual at an ancient oak — and gets kidnapped. _[WP]_
- [ ] **The whaling ship**: A decoy: Artemis puts Holly's locator beacon and a bomb on it. _[SS]_

**Key objects**

- [ ] **The Book of the People**: The fairies' secret rulebook, written in Gnommish. Artemis translates it with computer software. _[SS; WP]_
- [ ] **The acorn**: Fairies restore their magic by taking an acorn from an ancient oak near a river bend under a full moon and planting it somewhere else. _[WP]_
- [ ] **One ton of 24-carat gold**: Artemis's ransom demand for Holly. _[WP; SS]_
- [ ] **Time-stop**: Fairy technology that freezes time around Fowl Manor during the siege. _[SS; WP]_
- [ ] **The blue rinse**: A biological bomb that destroys living things but leaves buildings standing. _[SS]_
- [ ] **Tranquilizer dart gun**: Butler uses it to capture Holly. _[WP]_
- [ ] **Sedative (sleeping pills) in champagne**: Artemis's trick for slipping out of the time-stop. _[SS; WP]_
- [ ] **Foaly's tinfoil hat**: Worn to block imagined spying. _[SS-CL]_

**Themes**

- **Brains vs. brawn**: Artemis wins with planning and cleverness, while others rely on strength or magic.
- **Family**: Behind the crime, Artemis is trying to save his broken family: his missing father and sick mother.
- **Good guys and bad guys**: The 'villain' is the main character, and both sides do good and bad things.
- **Magic vs. technology**: Fairy magic goes up against human gadgets, and Foaly's science matches Artemis's.
- **Humans and nature**: The fairies hid underground to get away from humans, who harm the Earth.

**Plot outline**

- [ ] **Prologue – Ch. 2 — The Book**: Artemis (age 12) and Butler go to Ho Chi Minh City. Their contact, Nguyen, leads them to a fairy healer. Artemis gets her Book of the People and gives her a shot that cures her drinking. Back at Fowl Manor in Ireland, Artemis translates the Gnommish Book. His father is missing after a shipping disaster, and his mother stays shut in her room. _[SS; SS-CH]_
- [ ] **Ch. 3 – 4 — Holly and the Abduction**: Captain Holly Short of LEP Recon is sent after a troll that has escaped to the surface. She saves the humans, but it uses up her magic. Commander Root orders her to do the Ritual, so she goes to Ireland. Artemis and Butler have been watching the likely Ritual sites. Butler hits her with a tranquilizer dart while she's renewing her magic, and they take her back to Fowl Manor. _[SS; WP; parent review]_
- [ ] **Ch. 5 – 6 — Missing in Action / Siege**: The LEP realizes Holly has been kidnapped. Root follows her locator beacon to a whaling ship, but Artemis planted it there with a bomb, and Root barely escapes. Holly wakes at Fowl Manor, and Artemis tricks her into thinking she spilled fairy secrets under truth serum. The LEP surrounds the manor, and Butler beats the officers they send in. The fairies start a time-stop, and Artemis demands one ton of gold, saying he knows how to escape the time-stop. _[SS; WP; parent review]_
- [ ] **Ch. 7 – 8 — Mulch and the Troll**: Root sends Mulch Diggums, a dwarf thief who can enter houses uninvited, to tunnel in. Mulch finds Artemis's copy of the Book, then fakes his own death and escapes. Holly breaks her cell floor, finds soil, and does the Ritual to get her magic back. Cudgeon takes over and sends a troll into the manor. Butler nearly dies, but Holly heals him and he defeats the troll. Root is put back in charge. _[SS; WP]_
- [ ] **Ch. 9 – Epilogue — The Escape**: The fairies hand over the gold. Holly takes half of it back in exchange for granting Artemis a wish. Artemis, Butler, and Juliet drink sedative-laced champagne. The fairies set off the blue rinse, but when they try to enter, they get sick, which proves Artemis is alive. By falling asleep, the humans slipped out of the time-stop. The fairies leave, and Artemis's wish comes true: his mother is healed. The epilogue shows the story is Dr. Argon's case file. _[SS; WP]_

## Bunnicula — Deborah and James Howe

### Fact sheet

> Harold, the Monroe family's dog, tells the story. The Monroes find a small rabbit at a movie theater while watching Dracula and bring him home. Chester the cat reads lots of horror books, and he decides the bunny, Bunnicula, is a vampire: he sleeps all day, has fangs, and vegetables start turning white. Chester tries garlic, a 'steak' through the heart, and more to stop him, until everything ends in chaos and a trip to the vet.

**Characters**

- [ ] **Harold** (Narrator (dog)): The easygoing, snack-loving family dog who tells the story. _[SS; SS-CL]_
- [ ] **Chester** (Cat): Well-read tabby cat who loves horror and mystery books; sure Bunnicula is a vampire. _[SS-CL]_
- [ ] **Bunnicula** (The rabbit): Tiny rabbit with black markings like a cape and pointy fangs; sleeps all day. _[SS-CL; WP]_
- [ ] **Toby Monroe** (Younger son): Kind, playful younger son; shares late-night snacks with Harold. _[SS-CL]_
- [ ] **Pete Monroe** (Older son): Older son; argues with Toby and likes to show off. _[SS-CL]_
- [ ] **Mr. Monroe** (Father): An English professor whose many books Chester reads. _[SS-CL]_
- [ ] **Mrs. Monroe** (Mother): The family peacemaker; she names the bunny Bunnicula. _[SS; SS-CL]_

**Settings**

- [ ] **The movie theater**: Where the Monroes find the bunny while watching Dracula. _[SS; WP]_
- [ ] **The Monroe house**: Most of the story happens here, especially the living room and kitchen. _[SS]_
- [ ] **The vet's office**: Where the family takes the pets at the end. _[SS; WP]_

**Key objects**

- [ ] **The note**: Tied to the bunny; written in an old dialect from the Carpathian Mountains. _[WP; KIRKUS]_
- [ ] **White vegetables**: Vegetables drained of juice and turned white, each with two tiny holes. The first is a tomato, and lettuce and zucchini follow. _[WP; KIRKUS; SEARCH]_
- [ ] **Garlic**: Chester spreads it around the house to keep the 'vampire' away. _[SS; WP]_
- [ ] **Raw steak**: Chester mixes up 'stake' and 'steak' and tries to use a steak on Bunnicula. _[SS; WP]_
- [ ] **Chester's books**: Horror and vampire books Chester reads; they feed his theory. _[SS; SS-CL]_
- [ ] **Carrot juice / vegetable juice**: The vet puts the starving Bunnicula on a liquid diet and gives him carrot juice. It works so well that the Monroes keep feeding him vegetable juice. _[SS; WP; parent review]_

**Themes**

- **Jumping to conclusions**: Chester's imagination runs wild, and his 'evidence' leads him to do silly and harmful things.
- **Friendship**: Harold and Chester's friendship is tested when they disagree about the bunny.
- **Jealousy of the new arrival**: The vet says Chester's trouble is a kind of sibling rivalry with the new pet.
- **Kindness to someone different**: Harold chooses to help the strange little bunny instead of fearing him.

**Plot outline**

- [ ] **Editor's Note – Ch. 1 — The Arrival**: The book is presented as a manuscript written by a dog. On a dark and stormy night, the Monroes find a tiny bunny with a note at a showing of Dracula. Mrs. Monroe names him Bunnicula. _[SS; WP; Editor's Note (parent review)]_
- [ ] **Ch. 2 – 3 — Suspicion**: Pete and Toby fight over caring for the bunny, and Harold feels left out. Chester notices that the bunny's markings look like a cape and that he sleeps all day. Using his books, Chester begins to suspect a vampire. _[SS]_
- [ ] **Ch. 3 – 4 — The White Vegetables**: Chester tells Harold that Bunnicula has been getting out of his cage. Mr. Monroe finds the first drained vegetable, a white tomato. Later, Harold joins Chester's night watch. They see Bunnicula leaving the kitchen and find a white zucchini, and Harold starts taking Chester's vampire idea more seriously. _[SS; SS-CH; parent review]_
- [ ] **Ch. 5 – 6 — Chester Takes Action**: More vegetables turn white, and Chester tries and fails to warn the Monroes that Bunnicula is a vampire. He puts garlic around the bunny's cage and gets a bath for smelling of it. Then he mixes up 'stake' and 'steak' and attacks Bunnicula with a raw steak. Finally he tries water, and his behavior gets him shut outside. _[SS; WP; parent review]_
- [ ] **Ch. 7 – 9 — Starvation, Salad, and the Vet**: Harold figures out that Chester is keeping Bunnicula away from food on purpose, and the bunny grows weak. Harold brings Bunnicula to the salad, Chester attacks, and the dinner table turns to chaos. The family takes the pets to the vet. The vet gives Bunnicula carrot juice, and Chester starts seeing a cat psychiatrist. _[SS; WP; parent review]_

### Fact sheet

> Newt Newman is a quiet 10-year-old who feels invisible next to his big brother Chris, the star quarterback in their town of Appleton. When Chris is knocked into a coma during the Big Game, Newt's friends Cecil and JJ help him turn Chris's old football clothes into a Halloween costume, and Captain Nobody is born. Newt keeps wearing it and finds the courage to help people, stop a robbery, and learn the truth about his brother's accident.

### Fact sheet

> Jeremy Thatcher loves to draw, but his art teacher, Mr. Kravitz, is always criticizing him. Running from bullies and from Mary Lou Hutton, who has a crush on him, Jeremy finds Elives' Magic Supplies and buys a colorful sphere for a quarter. It's a dragon egg. The baby dragon, Tiamat, hatches under the full moon, shares thoughts with Jeremy, and grows fast. Jeremy has to keep her secret, care for her, and finally send her home to the dragons' world on Midsummer Night.
